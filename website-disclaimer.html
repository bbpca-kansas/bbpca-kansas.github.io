Website Disclaimer

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Summary

This independent website publishes education, commentary, advocacy, and opinion about beauty, barbering, consumer information, and public policy. Its content is for general informational and educational purposes—not legal or professional advice or official government guidance. Content may be incomplete or out of date.

About the content

BBPCA content may include factual summaries, analysis, policy positions, editorials, legislative updates, links, and educational material. We aim to identify sources for factual claims, but do not guarantee that all content is complete, current, or free of error. Laws, rules, and public records may change; consult current primary sources and qualified professionals for advice about a specific situation.

BBPCA is an independent advocacy group. Unless a page says otherwise, BBPCA's advocacy positions and commentary are not official positions of a government agency, licensing board, professional association, employer, educational institution, political organization, or regulatory authority. Statements identified as opinions or policy positions reflect the views of BBPCA or the named contributor.

No legal or professional advice

Nothing on this website is legal, professional, or regulatory advice, or an official government determination. Readers should consult applicable primary sources, agencies, and qualified professionals about their own circumstances. BBPCA is not a law firm. Visiting this site or contacting BBPCA does not create an attorney-client relationship. Do not send confidential information expecting it to be protected by attorney-client privilege.

Third-party materials and links

Some pages link to or quote materials created by others. Those materials remain subject to their owners' rights. A link or excerpt does not imply endorsement of every viewpoint or claim in the source. The legal basis for any particular quotation or other use depends on the circumstances; this disclaimer does not assert that every use qualifies for a particular exception. External sites and services are operated by others, and BBPCA does not control their content, availability, or policies.

Advocacy and decisions

BBPCA may advocate for legislative, regulatory, or policy changes affecting beauty and barbering professionals and consumers. Advocacy content expresses policy positions intended to support public discussion; it is not neutral government guidance. Readers are responsible for evaluating information and conducting their own research before relying on it.
