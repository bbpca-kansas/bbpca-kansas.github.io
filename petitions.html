Kansas consumer advocacy

Four petitions.
One stronger voice.

Each petition focuses on a distinct change BBPCA is asking Kansas leaders to consider. Read the requests below and check back for the signing forms.

Signing forms are not live yet. We’re preparing separate forms for each petition. No signatures are being collected on this page at this time.

Petition 01

Repeal SB 30

Ask Kansas lawmakers to repeal SB 30 and preserve a process for occupational licensing decisions that draws on agency expertise and public input.

Signing form coming soon

Petition 02

Right to know

Ask for clear disclosure before a beauty service when it is not licensed or regulated by the state, so consumers can make informed choices.

Signing form coming soon

Petition 03

Restore fair representation

Ask for fair and meaningful representation of licensed professionals on the Kansas Board of Cosmetology, including representation that reflects the professions it oversees.

Signing form coming soon

Petition 04

A separate board for body art

Ask Kansas to establish a dedicated board for body art, with oversight and representation focused on that profession.

Signing form coming soon
