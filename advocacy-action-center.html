Kansas consumer advocacy

Advocacy Action Center

Make Your Voice Heard

Beauty & Barber Professionals Consumer Advocates (BBPCA) works to protect consumer safety, professional standards, fair representation, and informed policy decisions within Kansas licensed professions.

Professionals, students, business owners, and consumers can use the action items below to show support for important issues affecting beauty, barbering, cosmetology, body art, education, and professional regulation.

Adding your name takes less than a minute and helps demonstrate public support to policymakers and decision-makers.

Current actions

Occupational licensing

Repeal SB 30

Support restoring a balanced occupational licensing process that benefits from professional expertise, agency knowledge, and public input.

Consumer protection

Restore Fair Representation

Support meaningful representation of licensed professionals on the Kansas Board of Cosmetology and other professional licensing boards.

Body art oversight

Dedicated Body Art Board

Support creating a dedicated regulatory board focused on the unique needs, safety requirements, and professional representation of Kansas body art professionals.

Why Advocacy Matters

Strong professional standards help protect consumers, support workforce development, and ensure that industry decisions include those with real-world experience.

The Advocacy Action Center allows professionals and consumers to participate in the legislative process by demonstrating support for policies that promote safety, accountability, education, and fair representation.
