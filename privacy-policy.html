Website policies

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Kansas Beauty and Barber Professionals Consumer Advocates (BBPCA) values your privacy. This policy describes information shared through the BBPCA website and the email, mailing-list, and form services linked from it.

Information you choose to provide

If you email us, join the mailing list, or submit a linked form, the information may include your name or preferred name, email address, message, and any responses or other information you choose to provide. The fields collected by a form depend on that form's settings. Please review the form before submitting and do not send sensitive, confidential, or unnecessary personal information.

Information processed by website and analytics providers

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like other hosting providers, GitHub may automatically process technical information, including IP addresses and request information, to deliver and protect the site. That processing is governed in part by GitHub's policies and systems.

GoatCounter is used on the homepage, Resource Library, Consumer Watch, Advocacy Action Center, and 2027 Priorities page to measure page views and selected interactions. Interactions include article openings, copy-link clicks, action-button clicks, and clicks on external resource links. Event names use public page titles and identifiers. The site code does not send form responses or search terms to these events; URLs containing query strings are not counted, and page-view referrers are limited to their origin. An action-button click does not tell us whether you completed a form.

We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site advertising trackers. GoatCounter is configured for aggregate site measurement and no-session interaction events. Google Forms, Gmail, Google Groups, and other external services may process information or use technologies under their own settings and privacy notices; we do not control those services.

How information is used and shared

We may use information you provide to:

  • Send mailing-list updates and respond to questions, tips, or inquiries.
  • Review feedback and understand recurring consumer or industry concerns.
  • Maintain and administer BBPCA communications.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We use hosting, email, mailing-list, and form providers to operate these services, and they may process information under their own policies. We may disclose information when required by law. We will not publish your name or identifying details with a personal story or tip without your explicit written permission.

We may review responses for recurring themes and discuss them in aggregate where appropriate. We do not promise that information submitted through a form is anonymous.

Retention and your choices

You may unsubscribe from mailing-list messages using the unsubscribe instructions provided with them. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you sent directly to BBPCA, email bbpconsumeradvocates@gmail.com. We will review requests for information we control. Information held by Google, GitHub, or another provider, including provider logs or backups, is also subject to that provider's systems and policies and may not be immediately removable by BBPCA.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to bbpconsumeradvocates@gmail.com.
